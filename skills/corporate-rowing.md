# Corporate rowing at Akademiska Roddföreningen

A three-hour rowing session for a team, at the academic rowing club on Brunnsviken (Royal National City Park, Stockholm). No experience needed; one club instructor rides in every boat.

- Page: https://event.akademiskarodd.se/ (Swedish: https://event.akademiskarodd.se/sv/)
- Group size: 4 to 20 participants, in 4- and 8-seat boats.
- When: weekdays, about 3 hours, open-water season.
- Programme: welcome and changing, rowing-machine introduction, rowing together on the lake, a friendly race between boats, fika (coffee and something sweet).
- Prices (fika included): 4 people 3 200 kr; 5–8 6 400 kr; 9–12 9 600 kr; 13–16 12 800 kr; 17–20 16 000 kr. Other group sizes on request.
- Weather: sessions run in rain; cancelled and refunded only if unsafe (for example strong wind).
- Bring: tight-fitting training clothes, flat sports shoes, water, a towel and dry clothes. Changing rooms and showers on site.
- Address: Frescati hagväg 18 A, 114 19 Stockholm, a short walk from T-bana Universitetet (red line).

## How to book

1. Send the booking form: https://www.svenskalag.se/akademiskaroddforeningen/formular/corporate-rowing/28710
2. Or email event@akademiskarodd.se with company, preferred dates, number of participants, contact person and any allergies.
3. The club replies to confirm a date and the format.

Do not promise a date: availability is confirmed by the club.
