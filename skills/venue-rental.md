# Party venue: the boathouse on Brunnsviken

A glass-walled hall on the upper floor of the rowing club's boathouse, with a terrace over the lake, in the Royal National City Park, Stockholm.

- Page: https://hyralokal.akademiskarodd.se/ (Swedish: https://hyralokal.akademiskarodd.se/sv/)
- Space: hall of about 55 m², terrace of 60 m², lounge, kitchen, cloakroom, two toilets.
- Capacity: at most 50 people inside; one long table seats 35, two long tables about 50.
- Season: May to October. Rental time 15:00 to 11:00 the next day.
- Prices: Saturday 15 000 kr; Sunday to Friday 12 000 kr; deposit 2 000 kr, refunded after inspection. Earlier access on weekdays 1 000 kr per hour (weekends not before 15:00); late departure after 11:00 1 000 kr per hour.
- Included: kitchen (dishwasher, four-plate stove, microwave, coffee maker), fridge and freezer, plates, cutlery and glasses for 50, tables and chairs (also usable on the terrace), Bluetooth speaker.
- Rules: private parties only, renter at least 25 years old; no smoking inside or on the terrace; barbecue only on the lawn by the water; no rain cover provided (free-standing tent allowed, no nails); clean up and leave by 11:00. Club members may use the boat hall, changing rooms and toilets during the rental.
- Full terms (Swedish PDF): https://www.akademiskarodd.se/dokument/37e8c5ff-8c24-49c3-a6ce-6708cafc8e61
- Address: Frescati hagväg 18 A, 114 19 Stockholm.

## How to book

1. Check free dates in the calendar (dates marked "Uthyrt" are taken): https://www.akademiskarodd.se/akademiskaroddforeningen-hyrafestlokal/kalender
2. Send the request form: https://www.svenskalag.se/akademiskaroddforeningen/formular/hyra-festlokal-deposition/27509
3. The club confirms by email, usually within a few days. The 2 000 kr deposit is due within 7 days of confirmation, the rent at the latest 30 days before the party; the door code follows payment.
4. Questions: hyralokal@akademiskarodd.se

Do not promise a date: availability is confirmed by the club.
